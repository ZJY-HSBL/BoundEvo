# BoundEvo v0.14.0 — Calibrated DE and Self-Adaptive jDE Research

This release revisits the apparent advantage of ABC from v0.13.0 with two **substantially more informative classical differential-evolution controls**: an independently calibrated DE/best/1/bin strategy and a self-adaptive, individual-parameter jDE implementation.

**The result is a negative/qualifying research finding, not a failed experiment:** ABC's previous 29/29 five-seed mean-error wins against one untuned DE/rand/1/bin parameter setting **did not generalize into a clear advantage** over the calibrated or self-adaptive DE settings in this new fresh-seed function comparison. All 822 original runs and 82,200 measured objective checkpoints were retained and published.

## Two deliberately separate research phases

**Calibration, not evaluation:** Four development functions F3/F10/F20/F30, three calibration seeds each, and six fixed conventional DE configurations (two strategies × three F/CR pairs): **72 true runs, 7,200 original best-so-far checkpoints**. Select the candidate with best average mean-error rank across the four development functions; apply deterministic candidate-order tie breaking.

Development winner: **DE/best/1/bin, F=0.5, CR=0.9** (`best050cr090`, mean development rank 1.500/6). The frozen winner, six-rank table and complete original outcomes are archived separately from evaluation.

**Evaluation, not tuning:** Remaining 25 retained official CEC2017 functions, 30D, **10 fresh paired seeds per function**, 10,000 function evaluations per run, original ABC vs development-calibrated DE/best/1/bin vs conventional jDE/rand/1/bin: **750 true runs, 75,000 measured best-so-far checkpoints**. Matched algorithms used identical sampled initial populations under the same seed.

**Important distinction:** The 25 evaluation functions were excluded from **this study's DE hyperparameter selection** but were already observed in BoundEvo v0.13.0. They must **not** be described as previously unseen research problems. Calibration and evaluation source commits differ due solely to a CI import/aggregation recovery: byte-identical core optimizer and benchmark files were verified before evaluation.

## Original observed outcomes

| Method | Mean function rank (lower better) | Function wins (lowest 10-seed mean error) | Hosted-worker mean seconds/run |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|
| ABC | 2.2000 | 8/25 | 3.134 |
| **Calibrated DE/best/1/bin** | **1.8400** | **9/25** | 2.014 |
| jDE | 1.9600 | 8/25 | **1.832** |

- ABC beat the calibrated DE in 11/25 function-level 10-seed **mean-error directions** (14 losses), two-sided Holm-adjusted exact sign **p=0.69004**.
- ABC beat jDE in 9/25 such directions (16 losses), Holm **p=0.45905**.
- Three-method function-block Friedman **Q=1.6800**, 20,000 random-label permutations **p=0.45078**.
- These statistics **do not support declaring any method superior** in this evaluation cohort. The more competitive fixed/adaptive DE strategies narrowed or eliminated ABC's previous observed advantage.
- Hosted runner timings are measurements, **not hardware-normalized**. FEs were equal but CPU time and algorithm generation counts are not directly comparable.

## New implementation and four scientific graphics

- Standard synchronous `DE/best/1/bin` with elitist mutation base, fixed F/CR, forced mutant crossover coordinate, target-wise greedy survival and box projection, with exact fixed FE accounting.
- Classical **jDE** self-adaptive `DE/rand/1/bin` according to Brest et al., IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation 10(6), 2006, [DOI 10.1109/TEVC.2006.872133](https://doi.org/10.1109/TEVC.2006.872133). Individual F and CR parameters are proposed with probabilities 0.1 each and inherited only after successful offspring selection. It is not SHADE or L-SHADE.
- Four source-derived SVGs: [DE calibration candidate ranks](https://github.com/ZJY-HSBL/BoundEvo/blob/v0.14.0/docs/figures/cec2017-tuned-de-development-ranks.svg); [25-function evaluation heatmap](https://github.com/ZJY-HSBL/BoundEvo/blob/v0.14.0/docs/figures/cec2017-tuned-de-holdout-heatmap.svg); [method rank/win summary](https://github.com/ZJY-HSBL/BoundEvo/blob/v0.14.0/docs/figures/cec2017-tuned-de-holdout-ranks.svg); [measured convergence median/IQRs](https://github.com/ZJY-HSBL/BoundEvo/blob/v0.14.0/docs/figures/cec2017-tuned-de-holdout-convergence.svg).
- The bilingual README research sections are regenerated from raw source tables and guarded by exact record coverage, source provenance and pytest+Ruff checks.

## Reproducibility and scientific limits

[Complete 822-run report and original data](https://github.com/ZJY-HSBL/BoundEvo/blob/v0.14.0/docs/experiments/cec2017-calibrated-de-jde-30d-10k-2026-10-21/report.md) · [Frozen split/tuning/inference protocol](https://github.com/ZJY-HSBL/BoundEvo/blob/v0.14.0/docs/calibrated_de_jde_protocol.md) · [Implementation methodology and published jDE references](https://github.com/ZJY-HSBL/BoundEvo/blob/v0.14.0/docs/de_jde_methods.md) · [English README](https://github.com/ZJY-HSBL/BoundEvo/blob/v0.14.0/README.md) · [中文 README](https://github.com/ZJY-HSBL/BoundEvo/blob/v0.14.0/README_CN.md)

The 25 functions are **calibration-excluded**, not fully blinded historically. Only six conventional DE candidates and a classical jDE implementation were evaluated: this is not a comparison against well-tuned SHADE/L-SHADE or all state-of-the-art black-box methods. Experiments use a pinned C-derived Python reference CEC2017 port rather than the organizers' original C executable. Ten seeds per function do not confer high power for small per-function differences. Statistical claims are function-block exploratory and do not generalize automatically to other benchmark families.

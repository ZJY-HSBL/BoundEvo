# BoundEvo v0.11.0 — Matched 10k-to-100k Budget Validation

This release extends the independent F16/F22 CEC2017 20-seed holdout from 10,000 to **100,000 function evaluations per run**. It tests algorithm rankings under a longer budget without pretending that reusing the same seeds creates independent new observations.

## 120 complete real 100k-FE trials

- CEC2017 F16 and F22, 30 dimensions, ABC / EDBF / RE, 20 paired seeds for each method/function.
- **120 completed real 100k-FE trials**, comprising 30 previously verified five-seed continuations and **90 newly executed** runs.
- **13,440 recorded convergence checkpoints**. Each of the 120 trajectories reproduces the corresponding historically recorded 10k objective error at FE=10,000, with budget, seed, numerical and monotonicity validation.
- Reference-derived CEC2017 Python port pinned to `424a9fa2757914c3e4cfdd8f59a268b1aeb3197f` (not the organizers' official C executable).
- Fixed optimizer hyperparameters N=100, M=15, K=5, L=1; seeded runs repeated 0–19.
- Real-data-derived plots and bilingual README tables regenerated from committed CSV evidence.

### Mean objective error at FE=100,000 (lower is better)

| Function | ABC | EDBF | RE |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|
| F16 | **1,509.14** | 2,094.03 | 1,817.86 |
| F22 | **4,900.70** | 6,087.92 | 5,012.80 |

ABC has the smallest observed mean error for both functions, but the F22 differences are variable and **do not establish statistical superiority**.

### Paired exact sign tests (two-sided, Holm-adjusted across four comparisons)

| Contrast | ABC wins/losses | Holm-adjusted p |
|:---|---:|---:|
| F16 ABC vs EDBF | 20 / 0 | 0.00000763 |
| F16 ABC vs RE | 18 / 2 | 0.00120735 |
| F22 ABC vs EDBF | 12 / 8 | 1.0 |
| F22 ABC vs RE | 9 / 11 | 1.0 |

Reported uncertainty also includes 10,000 reproducible paired-index bootstrap resamples, with 95% percentile intervals for mean errors and paired log1p advantage.

### Interpretation boundaries

**This is an exploratory adaptive extension, not an independent confirmatory validation.** The extension from 5 to 20 seeds was chosen after observing the high-variance F22 five-seed results. The 20 seed IDs are the same as v0.10's 10k cohort and should be treated as matched budget continuations, not 20 novel seeds. The four within-extension Holm-adjusted tests do not account for all research choices made across earlier stages.

Only F16/F22 were studied at this 100k budget; the results do not reproduce the original paper's full 29-function study, certify the original benchmark C binary, or demonstrate universal algorithm superiority.

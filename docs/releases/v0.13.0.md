# BoundEvo v0.13.0 — Complete CEC2017 Full-suite Differential Evolution Comparison

This research release introduces a real, conventional **generational DE/rand/1/bin** control and the first newly seeded **29/29-function** head-to-head comparison of ABC, ABC-Reverse and DE in BoundEvo.

## 435 newly executed original CEC2017 optimization trials

- Every retained CEC2017 official function: F1 and F3–F30 (F2 excluded), D=30.
- **Three methods:** `abc`, `abc_reverse`, `de_rand1bin`.
- **Five completely new paired seed IDs/function, exactly 10,000 objective evaluations/run.** The three algorithms started with the identical original sampled population on every matched function/seed.
- **29 × 3 × 5 = 435 genuinely new optimization trials**, independent of previous 200- and 144-run experiments, with **43,500 real original best-so-far checkpoint records**.
- Full original trials/history CSV, 87 method/function summaries with 10,000-resample seed-bootstrap mean intervals, 87 function ranks, three Holm-corrected function-level sign tests, a 20,000-resample randomized-label function-block Friedman test, strict six-shard provenance, bilingual README interpretation, and three measured-data scientific SVG plots.

### Measured global outcome

| Algorithm | Mean rank over 29 functions (lower better) | Functions with lowest 5-seed mean error |
|:---|---:|---:|
| **ABC** | **1.0690** | **27/29** |
| ABC-Reverse | 2.1034 | 2/29 |
| DE/rand/1/bin | 2.8276 | 0/29 |

ABC's five-seed mean error was smaller than DE's on **all 29 functions** in this fixed-configuration study; ABC-Reverse achieved the lowest mean at F1 and F17.

- Function-block Friedman statistic: **Q=45.3103**; randomized-label Monte Carlo permutation p≈**0.0000499975** (20,000 permutations, including +1 correction; this is an upper resolution limit when no permutation exceeds Q).
- Across-function paired sign test, ABC vs DE: ABC wins 29/29 function mean comparisons, **Holm p≈1.12×10⁻⁸**.
- ABC vs ABC-Reverse: ABC wins 27/29, **Holm p≈3.25×10⁻⁶**.
- ABC-Reverse vs DE: Reverse wins 24/29, **Holm p≈0.000546**.
- Direction of five-seed function means is *not* the same as high-power per-function seed-level significance.

### Measured runtime trade-off

Mean **per-run wall-clock time over the 29 functions** in these six hosted Actions shards: ABC ≈**2.29 s**, ABC-Reverse ≈**2.31 s**, DE ≈**1.30 s**. This conventional DE implementation generally runs faster per fixed 10k FE budget on the sampled runner environments, despite higher average errors. **Timing is observational** and not hardware-normalized; do not infer general efficiency rankings from these runners.

## Standard differential evolution control

The new `boundevo.de_baseline.differential_evolution_rand1bin` implements **generational DE/rand/1/bin** with NP=100, difference weight F=0.8, binomial crossover CR=0.9, forced mutant coordinate, one-to-one greedy selection and box projection. It has deterministic seeds and **strict exact 10k-FE accounting**: 100 initial evaluations + 99 full generations of 100. It is a standard baseline implementation, **not an original optimization-method contribution**.

## Methodological qualifications

- **Five new seeds/function** give broad function coverage but insufficient power to resolve small individual-function effects.
- CEC2017 is a curated benchmark, not a random sample of all optimization problems. Block permutation assumes function-level algorithm-label exchangeability; p-values do **not** demonstrate universal optimization dominance.
- DE used **one fixed, untuned parameter configuration**. Results should not be interpreted as ABC outperforming the full DE family or well-tuned/current state-of-the-art optimizers.
- Equal **function-evaluation budgets** do not equate CPU cost, population generation count, or operator overhead.
- Scientific objectives use the pinned independent **C-derived Python CEC2017 reference port** at commit `424a9fa2757914c3e4cfdd8f59a268b1aeb3197f`; results are not an evaluation of the organizers' official C binary.

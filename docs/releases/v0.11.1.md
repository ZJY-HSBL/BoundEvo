# BoundEvo v0.11.1 — Real cross-function validation and mechanistic ablation

This research release extends v0.11.0 with **200 newly executed optimization trials**, an independent-seed cross-function cohort and the new diagnostic coefficient-order permutation **ABC-Shuffle**. It also reanalyses 120 previously completed 100k-evaluation trials. These 120 existing trials are **not counted as new experiments**.

## New experimental evidence

**Independent-seed CEC2017 replication:** F3, F10, F20 and F30 at dimension 30, 10,000 evaluations per run, three fixed methods ABC/EDBF/RE, ten new paired seeds per function, **120 real optimizer trials**. ABC has the smallest observed mean objective gap on F3/F20/F30; RE has the smallest observed gap on F10. Paired Holm-adjusted exact sign tests favor ABC over each comparator at F3 and F30 (adjusted p=0.015625), but not at F10 or F20. Functions were selected after earlier research and this is exploratory, not a blinded full-suite confirmation.

**ABC-Shuffle mechanism ablation:** F10, F16, F20 and F22 at dimension 30 and 10,000 evaluations, ABC vs ABC-Shuffle with ten additional new paired seeds per function, **80 real optimizer trials**. Shuffling ABC's original coefficient positions preserves its complete coefficient multiset, affine sum, bounds and one-attempt generation; it alters the assignment of coefficient positions to elite-first ordered parents and subsequent random-number streams. ABC had lower observed mean errors on all four functions, but none of the four within-study Holm-adjusted exact paired sign tests reached significance. Thus coefficient/rank coupling is an **unconfirmed hypothesis**, not a proven causal advantage.

**Anytime-performance study (no new optimization trials):** reuse and fully revalidate all **120 existing 100k-evaluation F16/F22 trajectories**, including 13,440 original best-so-far checkpoints, to calculate log-budget integrated log1p objective loss, paired bootstrap confidence intervals, and pooled-baseline target attainment curves. On F16, the 10k–100k anytime sign test for ABC vs EDBF is Holm-significant (p≈0.000160); vs RE is not (p≈0.124). Results are exploratory, and the 100k cohort was adaptively selected.

## Original scientific figures

Six new source-data-generated scientific SVGs include median convergence with seed IQR bands, scatterplots of matched seed endpoints, empirical target-attainment curves, paired anytime forest-style confidence intervals, the new four-function seed replication figure and per-seed ABC-Shuffle ablation effects. Bilingual README embeds results, interpretation, and evidence links. Synthetic test fixtures are never used as published data.

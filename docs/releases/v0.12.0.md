# BoundEvo v0.12.0 — Coefficient-Order Mechanism Study with 144 New Runs

Building on the 200 genuine optimization trials in v0.11.1, this release adds **144 new matched-seed optimizer runs**, **3,312 newly recorded best-so-far checkpoints**, a **50,000-vector coefficient-position audit**, two more original-data SVG figures, and the diagnostic **ABC-Reverse** method. Across v0.11.0 → v0.12.0, 344 additional original optimizer trials have been completed. Earlier 120 long-budget runs reanalysed for anytime statistics are not counted as new trials. None of these studies establishes universal superiority.

## Research contributions

### 1. Fresh-seed cross-function robustness (120 new actual trials)

- Four CEC2017 functions F3/F10/F20/F30 at D=30, **10,000 function evaluations**,
  ABC/EDBF/RE, **10 previously unused paired seed IDs** per function.
- Fixed source-aligned population/parent settings and a pinned C-derived
  reference implementation.
- Original trial/checkpoint CSVs, source manifests, fixed protocols, bootstrap
  intervals for mean errors, and eight Holm-corrected paired sign tests.

| Function | ABC mean error | EDBF mean error | RE mean error |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|
| F3 | **39,676.77** | 208,700.89 | 137,012.63 |
| F10 | 7,888.77 | 7,575.24 | **7,501.22** |
| F20 | **1,056.71** | 1,102.81 | 1,089.25 |
| F30 | **86,467.38** | 51,194,451.61 | 5,362,836.92 |

The four F3/F30 ABC pairwise tests reached Holm-adjusted p=0.015625;
F10/F20 comparisons did not. **The algorithm ranking depends on the problem**;
the F10 observed mean leader is RE, not ABC.

### 2. Anytime research (reanalysis, no additional optimizer trials)

From the 120 *existing* F16/F22 100k-FE original trajectories, calculated
the sample-and-hold log-budget integral of log1p(best-so-far objective error)
over [10k,100k], together with paired bootstrap mean differences, seed-level
interquartile convergence ribbons and empirical target-attainment curves.

| Function | ABC anytime loss | EDBF | RE |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|
| F16 | **7.4796** | 7.7680 | 7.6524 |
| F22 | **8.2842** | 8.8065 | 8.6216 |

F16 ABC vs EDBF has Holm-adjusted p=0.000160 on anytime paired sign tests;
ABC vs RE is **not significant** (p=0.1242), despite a stronger
endpoint-only comparison from the earlier release. Endpoint ranking cannot
replace whole-trajectory analysis.

### 3. Mechanistic ABC-Shuffle ablation (80 more actual trials)

Original ABC sequentially samples constrained affine coefficients, while the
optimizer orders parents with elites first. New `abc_shuffle` **preserves the
exact coefficient multiset** but randomly permutes which parent receives each
coefficient. This is a targeted diagnostic of coefficient-position/elite-rank
coupling; the extra permutation changes later RNG streams.

- F10/F16/F20/F22, D=30, 10k FE, **10 other unused matched seeds per function**,
  ABC vs ABC-Shuffle, totaling **80 real optimizer runs** with
  **1,840 measured convergence checkpoints**.
- The original ABC produced smaller observed mean objective errors on all four
  functions, but **all four Holm-adjusted exact paired sign tests have p=1.0**.
  The hypothesized mechanism **is not statistically established**.

| Function | Original ABC mean | ABC-Shuffle mean |
|:---|---:|---:|
| F10 | **7,559.44** | 7,678.01 |
| F16 | **1,995.37** | 2,177.90 |
| F20 | **1,009.08** | 1,061.83 |
| F22 | **5,484.03** | 7,523.93 |

### 4. New ABC-Reverse control — 144 additional original optimizer runs

ABC-Reverse **deterministically reverses the original valid ABC coefficient vector** before assigning it to elite-first ordered parents. This conserves every coefficient, the sum and bounds, and the **RNG state** at each coefficient-generation call. It removes the extra random draw introduced by stochastic ABC-Shuffle. Different offspring still cause the optimizer populations to diverge, so this is a limited mechanism probe, not an experimental proof of causality.

- CEC2017 F10/F16/F20/F22, 30 dimensions, **10,000 FE per run**, ABC/ABC-Shuffle/ABC-Reverse, **12 new matched seed IDs per function** — 144 new optimizer trials.
- All **3,312 source convergence states** passed FE-budget, provenance, monotonicity, paired-initialization and endpoint checks.
- 8 paired exact sign tests jointly Holm-adjusted; **none reaches 0.05** after correction.

| Function | ABC mean error | ABC-Shuffle | ABC-Reverse |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|
| F10 | 7,701.73 | **7,504.50** | 7,965.95 |
| F16 | **2,097.11** | 2,133.39 | 2,372.11 |
| F20 | **965.09** | 989.04 | 1,062.09 |
| F22 | **6,006.29** | 6,503.67 | 8,064.01 |

The minimum adjusted p for a reverse comparison is **0.30859** (F16/F20); apparent mean-error differences must not be described as statistically proven effects.

A distinct **50,000-vector** audit of the original ABC sampler (M=15, five elite-parent positions) shows that the first five coefficient slots carry **42.23% of the mean relative absolute weight mass** (versus 33.33% of coefficient positions). This is evidence of descriptive positional skew, **not causal fitness improvement**. Reverse control and generator diagnostics identify a research question for future function-blind evaluation.

### Interpretation limitations

All findings are **exploratory**, because selected functions and earlier
extensions followed inspection of previous runs. Fresh *random seeds* do not
make function selection independent. The objective backend is a pinned
C-derived Python implementation rather than the official organizers' C binary.
Sample sizes, function coverage, study-stage selection and multiple testing
preclude broad generalization or proving causal algorithmic mechanisms.
All eight plots render original experiment data or freshly generated coefficient-sampler values only. No synthetic examples are included
in published research results.

Release assets include the Python wheel and source distribution.
